An always-on AirPlay receiver for Android TV, tuned for low-end TVs. Mirror an iPhone or Mac screen, stream music with cover art and controls, or cast a video from QuickTime, straight to the TV.

- Starts with the TV, wakes it when a sender connects, and takes the TV's own name
- Low-latency screen mirroring (H.264/H.265) rendered straight to the display
- AirPlay 2 sessions with HomeKit pairing, including macOS QuickTime file casting
- Music with title, artist, artwork, progress and play state, including from apps such as YouTube Music
- Pauses the previous device when another one takes over
- TV interface with remote navigation, a now-playing screen and an ambient mode tinted by the artwork
- AAC-ELD, AAC-LC and ALAC audio, HLS video with remote controls, picture-in-picture, optional PIN

Fork of jqssun/android-airplay-server, built on UxPlay. Not affiliated with Apple Inc.
